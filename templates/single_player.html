Single-Player Mode

Run a private capital fund on your own — your personal sandbox.

  1. Create your game — one short form sets up your firm and fund. You'll need an access code.
  2. Play at your own pace — you're the only team: search for companies, submit term sheets, win deals, and manage your portfolio.
  3. Advance instantly — each time you mark a phase complete on your dashboard, the simulation runs immediately. Play all 7 fund years in one sitting or over weeks.

Perfect for learning the ropes or testing a strategy before taking on other players.
